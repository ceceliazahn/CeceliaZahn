MY BUSINESS AND CURRENT PROJECT

ZAHNE

Building a modern fashion and lifestyle brand.

ZAHNE is a fashion and lifestyle brand I started in 2026 to combine my interests in fashion, design, entrepreneurship, and technology.

THE IDEA

ZAHNE began as an idea for a fashion and lifestyle brand inspired by the styles, trends, and online culture I see around me. I wanted to create something that felt current, personal, and fun to build while developing a business of my own.

THE BRAND

My goal is to create products that feel unique while building a recognizable brand that can continue to grow and evolve. I am developing ZAHNE's visual identity, product ideas, and overall aesthetic with the goal of creating a consistent experience across platforms.

BUILDING THE BRAND

I have been developing ZAHNE from the ground up, including its name, logo, product ideas, and online presence. I am exploring different products and styles while learning how to create a brand that connects with its intended audience. This process has given me hands-on experience with branding, design, product development, and entrepreneurship.

E-COMMERCE + AI

One of the things that makes ZAHNE especially interesting to me is the opportunity to experiment with AI and automation. I am exploring how technology can help with product development, content creation, marketing, organization, and other parts of running an online store.

I want to build a business that brings together fashion, digital commerce, and emerging technology. ZAHNE gives me a way to experiment with these ideas through a real project.

WHAT I'M LEARNING

Building ZAHNE has taught me that creating a business involves much more than having a good idea. I am learning how branding, design, marketing, technology, and business decisions connect. I am also learning how to take an idea from something in my head and turn it into something real.
