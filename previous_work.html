Entrepreneurship & E-commerce

Carriage House Works

Carriage House Works was my first experience running an online shop and one of my earliest projects in entrepreneurship. Through Etsy, I designed and sold picture frames while learning about branding, product development, marketing, photography, and the behind-the-scenes work of running an online business.

This experience taught me the importance of efficiency and optimization. By improving how I managed the business, I more than doubled its revenue year over year.

The experience helped shape my interest in building businesses and turning creative ideas into real products.
